💡 Listening on Mobile Devices?

Since your server runs locally, your mobile device needs a way to connect. We recommend using a tunneling service:

  • Tailscale: Connect your devices to your private tailnet and replace localhost:8000 in the URL above with your machine's Tailscale IP.
  • ngrok: Run ngrok http 8000 and replace the host above with your public ngrok URL.
